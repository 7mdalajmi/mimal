Mimal is a perfume formula lab. Photograph each oil on the scale and Mimal reads its name and weight into the formula. It works out the percentages, checks every material against IFRA limits, and keeps a version each time you edit. For iPhone and iPad, with no accounts and no ads; your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud.
